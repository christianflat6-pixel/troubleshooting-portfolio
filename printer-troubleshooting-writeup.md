# Troubleshooting Write-Up: Printer Printing Every Line Twice

**Author:** Christian Boakoh
**Equipment:** HP LaserJet P1102w, used with Windows computers
**Type of issue:** Hardware fault (print quality and paper feed)

## Problem

A printer in a hospital office was printing every line of text twice. Each letter had a faint shadow under it, which made documents hard to read. A paper had also become stuck inside the printer.

## Symptoms

- Double-image printing (shadow under all the text)
- Paper stuck inside the printer
- The printer was not pulling paper in properly

## Steps I Took

1. Opened the printer and took out the toner cartridge.
2. Cleaned the dust and loose toner from inside the printer.
3. Shook the toner cartridge gently so the toner spread evenly.
4. Found the paper stuck inside and removed it.
5. Checked the paper pickup roller (the round rubber roller that pulls paper into the printer). It was worn and could not grip the paper.
6. Replaced the roller.
7. Put the printer back together and printed a test page.

## Result

The printer printed clearly again, with no shadow or double text. Paper fed in normally and did not jam.

## Root Cause

- **Paper jam:** The worn pickup roller could not pull the paper in properly, which caused the paper to get stuck.
- **Double printing:** I believe the cause was toner and dust build-up inside the printer, which cleared after the cleaning and shaking the toner cartridge.

I did the cleaning and the roller replacement in one visit and did not test between each step, so I cannot say for certain which step fixed the shadow printing.

## What I Would Do Differently

- Change one thing at a time and print a test page after each step, so I know exactly which step solved the problem.
- Write down the printer model and the date of each repair so repeat problems are easier to spot.

## How To Prevent It

- Clean the inside of the printer regularly to stop dust and toner building up.
- Check the rollers for wear and replace them early, before they cause paper jams.
- Remove stuck paper carefully and completely, so no small pieces are left inside.

## Skills Shown

- Diagnosing a hardware fault from symptoms
- Hands-on printer repair (toner cartridge servicing, paper jam clearing, roller replacement)
- Finding the root cause of a problem and not only fixing what is visible
- Clear written documentation of a technical fix
